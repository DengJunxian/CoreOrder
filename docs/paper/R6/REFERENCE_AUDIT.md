## Provenance of the audit

The R4 Proposal DOCX distributed with the leader materials matches the standalone original by SHA-256 `58a0930a39159303b3aad3253923b8759e75bdb1c7778321054079cd41b2d8c7`. The R4 research route, `common/06_Reuse_License.md`, `common/references/hpc_reuse_matrix.csv`, `literature_review.md` and the R4 bibliography guided the selection. The publication metadata and cited claims were checked against the sources above. The three source pins were checked against the target commit's gitlinks and the local locked submodule objects. [Project upstream lock](../../../UPSTREAM.lock.json) · [Third-party notices](../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)

The selected platform and component versions are the ones present in the tested code. The related-work systems ABIDES and JAX-LOB are literature comparisons. The R4 screening also examined other simulator and HPC candidates. Runtime claims here come from the actual retained components, experiments, and implementation.
